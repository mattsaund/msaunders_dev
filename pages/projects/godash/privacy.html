Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

GoDash is made by one independent developer, Matthew Saunders. In this policy, “I,” “me” and “my” mean me, and “the app” means GoDash. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

The short version: your recordings, telemetry and account details are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded to me. I don’t run servers that collect personal data, and the app has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. Information leaves your device only when it goes directly to a service you choose to use: Apple’s services (Maps, Apple Music, podcast search, Sign in with Apple and the App Store), which are covered by Apple’s own privacy policy, and the hosts of any podcasts you play.

1. What the app accesses and why

  • Camera and microphone. The app uses your camera, and optionally your microphone, to record looping dash cam video and audio. Recordings are stored only on your device. I don’t receive, upload or have any access to them.
  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your location while you’re using it for navigation and for the speed, heading and trip data in the telemetry module. Location is processed on your device. When you search for a destination, your search text is sent to Apple’s Maps service to return results, and Apple handles it.
  • Motion and Fitness. The app uses your device’s motion sensors to calculate the g-force readings in the telemetry module and, if you turn it on, to overlay that data onto your saved clips. This is processed on your device.
  • Photos. If you export a clip, the app adds that video to your photo library. It only writes clips you choose to export. It does not read or scan your existing photos.
  • Apple Music. If you use the music module, the app uses Apple’s MusicKit to show your library and play music. Access to your Apple Music account and library is handled by Apple. Playing full tracks requires an Apple Music subscription.
  • Podcasts. Searching and browsing use Apple’s public podcast directory. When you open a show, the app loads its feed and streams or downloads episodes directly from that podcast’s host, which can see your IP address like any website. Your subscriptions and downloads are stored on your device and never sent to me.
  • Sign in with Apple. Signing in is optional, and the app records and saves clips either way. If you sign in, the name and email you share through Sign in with Apple are stored on your device, in the iOS Keychain, to show your profile. You can hide your email with Apple’s private relay. This information is never sent to any server of mine.
  • GoDash Pro. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active so it can unlock Pro features.
  • Diagnostics. Errors may be logged to your device’s console to help with debugging. The app has no third-party crash reporting or analytics, and these logs are not sent to me.

2. What I don’t collect

  • I don’t collect, store or sell your dash cam footage or telemetry.
  • I don’t use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or ad networks.
  • I don’t use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • I don’t track you across other apps or websites.

3. Storage and retention

Clips, telemetry, settings and account details are stored on your device. Clips are excluded from iCloud backup by default. You decide what’s kept:

  • Delete individual clips, or all of them, in the app.
  • The free tier automatically removes the oldest clips beyond its limit.
  • Sign out or delete your GoDash profile in the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

4. Apple services

The app relies on Apple services: MapKit (maps and search), MusicKit (Apple Music), Apple’s podcast directory (podcast search), Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit (the App Store). Information handled by these services is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing

I don’t sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. Your data stays on your device, so there is nothing for me to share. If you share a clip yourself with the app’s share button, it goes to whichever app or destination you pick.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke camera, microphone, location, motion, photos and Apple Music permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Delete your clips and your GoDash profile in the app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I’ll change the effective date above and post the new version at this address.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email me at matt.saunders.963@gmail.com.
