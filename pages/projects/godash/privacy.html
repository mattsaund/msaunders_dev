Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

GoDash is made by one independent developer, Matthew Saunders. In this policy, “I,” “me” and “my” mean me, and “the app” means GoDash. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

The short version: your recordings, telemetry and account details stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded to me. The app has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and I don’t sell anything about you. GoDash’s optional social features (a username, friends, groups, shared clips and shared trips) use Apple’s iCloud, and only when you use them. Group chats, shared clips and trips are visible only to the people in the group. Everything else goes straight to a service you choose to use: Apple’s services, which are covered by Apple’s own privacy policy, and the hosts of any podcasts you play.

1. What the app accesses and why

  • Camera and microphone. The app uses your camera, and optionally your microphone, to record looping dash cam video and audio. Recordings are stored only on your device. I don’t receive, upload or have any access to them. A clip leaves your device only if you share it (see sections 2 and 6).
  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your location while you’re using it for navigation, voice guidance, the speed, heading and trip data in the telemetry module, and finding places near you or along your route. Location is processed on your device. When you search for a place or ask for directions, your search and the start and end of the route are sent to Apple’s Maps service to return results, and Apple handles them. Your location is shared with other people only through the optional features in section 2, and only when you turn them on.
  • Motion and Fitness. The app uses your device’s motion sensors to calculate the g-force readings in the telemetry module, to detect a hard brake or a crash if you turn those on, and, if you choose, to overlay that data onto your saved clips. This is processed on your device.
  • Photos. If you export a clip, the app adds that video to your photo library. It only writes clips you choose to export. It does not read or scan your existing photos.
  • Maps data on your device. Your favorites, recent destinations, Home and Work, where you parked and your map settings are stored only on your device.
  • Apple Music. If you use the music module, the app uses Apple’s MusicKit to show your library, recommendations and charts and to play music. Access to your Apple Music account and library is handled by Apple. Playing full tracks requires an Apple Music subscription. Pinned items are stored on your device.
  • Podcasts. Searching and browsing use Apple’s public podcast directory. When you open a show, the app loads its feed and streams or downloads episodes, chapters and transcripts directly from that podcast’s host, which can see your IP address like any website. Your subscriptions, downloads, saved episodes and playback settings are stored on your device and never sent to me.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app sends notifications about friend requests, group messages and stops on a shared trip. They’re delivered through Apple’s Push Notification service, which Apple runs.
  • Sign in with Apple. Signing in is optional, and the app records and saves clips either way. If you sign in, the name and email you share through Sign in with Apple are stored on your device, in the iOS Keychain, to show your profile. You can hide your email with Apple’s private relay. Your email is never sent to any server of mine and is never shown to other users. If you also reserve a username, the name on your GoDash profile is shown next to it (see section 2).
  • GoDash Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you’ve bought Pro so it can unlock Pro features. Pro earned through an invite is described in section 2.
  • Diagnostics. Errors may be logged to your device’s console to help with debugging. The app has no third-party crash reporting or analytics, and these logs are not sent to me.

2. Social features and iCloud

GoDash’s social features are optional, and some are still rolling out. They need you to be signed in to iCloud. They’re stored with Apple’s CloudKit service in the app’s iCloud container. To tell your phone apart from others, the app uses an anonymous identifier that CloudKit gives it for your iCloud account. That identifier doesn’t reveal your Apple Account email, name or phone number, and the app never reads your contacts.

  • Your username. If you reserve a username, it’s stored in the app’s public iCloud database with your anonymous identifier, an avatar colour and the name on your GoDash profile, if you have one. Anyone using GoDash can look up your username, and it’s how friends find you and how your invite code works.
  • Friends and blocking. Friend requests, friendships and blocks are stored as small records in the same public database, holding only the two anonymous identifiers involved and the request’s status. You can remove a friend or block someone at any time. Blocked users can’t send you requests, see you on the map or share with you.
  • Sharing your drives. If you turn on “Share My Drives with Friends,” the app shares your position, direction and speed with your friends while you’re driving with GoDash open. It never shares while you’re parked or when the app is closed. Only one record is kept, overwritten as you drive and deleted when you stop sharing or close the app. Friends don’t see a position older than two minutes.
  • Groups and chat. A group is stored in the iCloud account of the person who created it and shared with its members using Apple’s iCloud sharing. Messages, member lists and group details are visible only to the group’s members. They aren’t visible to me or anyone else.
  • Shared clips. When you share a clip with a group, a short trimmed copy is encrypted on your phone before it’s uploaded. The key to unlock it is kept inside the group, so only the group’s members can play it. The encrypted file itself is stored in the app’s public iCloud database because it’s too big for most people’s iCloud storage, but without the key it can’t be watched, by me or by anyone outside the group.
  • Shared trips. If you join a trip with your group, the trip’s destination, route and stops, and your position, direction and estimated arrival, are shared with the group’s members while the trip is under way. Adding a stop notifies the others. Your position stops being shared when you leave the trip or it ends, and trip positions are deleted when the trip ends.
  • Invites and Pro rewards. Your invite code is your username. If you enter a friend’s code, a record linking your anonymous identifier to theirs is stored in the public database. A week later, if you’re still using GoDash, two records grant you both Pro for a set time. To check that you’re a new user, the app asks the App Store, on your device, when your Apple Account first downloaded GoDash. That date isn’t sent anywhere.
  • Reporting. You can report a user, message or clip. A report includes the reported content, the usernames involved and your reason, and it’s sent to me so I can review it. Reported content is hidden for you right away. I act on reports within 24 hours, which can include removing content and removing users who break the rules.

3. What I can see

I can see the records in the app’s public iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit tools, as can Apple: usernames and profile names, the anonymous identifiers in friend, invite and Pro records, any position currently being shared, reports, and encrypted clip files that I can’t open. I use this only to run the features, to review reports and to fix problems. I can’t see group messages, member lists, trips or the keys to shared clips, because they live in your and your group members’ own iCloud accounts. I can’t see anything stored only on your device, including your recordings.

4. What I don’t collect

  • I don’t collect, store or sell your dash cam footage or telemetry. A clip leaves your device only when you share it, and only in encrypted form.
  • I don’t keep a history of where you’ve driven. Shared positions are overwritten as you drive and deleted when sharing ends.
  • I don’t use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or ad networks.
  • I don’t use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • I don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • I don’t read your contacts.

5. Storage and retention

Clips, telemetry, settings, map places and account details are stored on your device. Clips are excluded from iCloud backup by default. Social data is kept in iCloud only for as long as it’s needed:

  • Delete individual clips, or all of them, in the app. The free tier automatically removes the oldest clips beyond its limit.
  • A shared drive position is deleted when you stop sharing or close the app. Trip positions are deleted when the trip ends.
  • Messages and shared clips stay in a group until the person who sent them deletes them or the group is deleted. Leaving a group removes your access to it.
  • Your username, friends and invite record stay until you delete them (see section 6).
  • Reports are kept for as long as I need them to review the report and keep the community safe.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Social data in iCloud stays until you delete it in the app first, or ask me to delete it.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete Username in Settings, or Delete Account in Profile, deletes your username and profile, your side of every friendship and block, any position you’re sharing, the invite code you entered and your own Pro grant. It also deletes the messages, clips and trip data you’ve shared in groups. After that, anyone can take the username.
  • Pro a friend has already earned from your invite stays theirs. That record holds only anonymous identifiers.
  • If you can’t use the app to delete your data, email me and I’ll delete it within 30 days.

7. Apple services

The app relies on Apple services: MapKit (maps, search, directions and Look Around), MusicKit (Apple Music), Apple’s podcast directory (podcast search), CloudKit and iCloud (the social features), the Apple Push Notification service, Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit (the App Store). Information handled by these services is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing

I don’t sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. What other GoDash users can see is only what you choose to share with them through the social features above. If you share a clip, a route or your arrival time with the system share button, it goes to whichever app or person you pick.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. You must be at least 13, or the minimum age in your country if that’s higher, to use the social features.

10. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke camera, microphone, location, motion, photos, Apple Music and notification permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Use GoDash without the social features. Recording, clips, maps, music and podcasts all work without a username.
  • Turn “Share My Drives with Friends” on or off, leave a group or a trip, remove friends and block people at any time.
  • Delete your clips, your username and social data, and your GoDash profile in the app.
  • Restore your Pro purchase from the app on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I’ll change the effective date above and post the new version at this address.
