Your Phone, Your Dash Cam

GoDash turns your iPhone into a dash cam, with navigation, live telemetry and your music on one screen.

Free. Requires iOS 18 or later.

Features

Loop recording

A rolling buffer runs the whole drive. One tap saves up to 10 minutes of video.

Built-in navigation

Turn-by-turn directions, stops and route choices, powered by Apple Maps.

Live telemetry

Speed, g-force, heading, trip distance and altitude at a glance, and on your clips if you want.

Apple Music and podcasts

Your music and podcasts play right inside the app, so you never switch apps while driving.

Save and share

Saved clips land in one list. Play them back, export to Photos or share to a group.

Crash detection

Can watch for a hard brake or a collision and save the clip on its own, no hands needed.

New in GoDash 3

Drive together.

Groups, group trips and a rebuilt app.

Groups

Make a group with up to 12 friends and share dashcam clips. Only your group can watch them.

  • Trim a clip to the moment
  • Encrypted before it leaves your phone
  • See friends on the map while they drive

Group trips

Start a trip and your group joins in one tap. Every car shows on the map.

  • When someone stops, the rest can stop too
  • Spoken alerts, so eyes stay on the road
  • Shared only while GoDash is open

A better map

Find places along your route, add stops and pick the fastest way.

  • Stops and route choices
  • Look Around and place cards
  • Voice guidance and live arrival times

Music and podcasts

More of Apple Music and your podcasts, right on the dashboard.

  • Artists, charts and your queue
  • Chapters, transcripts and any speed
  • Podcasts keep playing in the background

A cleaner dashboard

Tabs along the bottom and every control one tap away.

  • Recording status around the Dynamic Island
  • Every camera setting in one menu
  • Save a clip in one tap

Invite friends, get Pro

Your username is your invite code. When a new friend joins and keeps driving for a week, you both get 6 months of Pro.

Private by design

Your recordings and telemetry stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share a clip, and shared clips are encrypted so only your group can watch them. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and I don't run any servers that collect your data.
