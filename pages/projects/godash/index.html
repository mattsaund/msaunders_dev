Your Phone, Your Dash Cam

GoDash turns your iPhone into a smart dashcam. loop recording, navigation, live telemetry, and your music, all in one clean view.

Loop recording

A continuous rolling buffer runs the whole time you drive. Tap once to save up to 10 minutes of video with speed, g-force, heading, and altitude baked onto the footage.

Built-in navigation

Turn-by-turn directions with a clean driving mini map. Powered by Apple Maps.

Live telemetry

Speed, g-force, heading, trip distance, and altitude at a glance.

Apple Music

Fully featured Apple Music without leaving the app. Prevents app switching while driving so you keep your eyes on the road.

Save & export

Every clip lands in a tidy list you can play back on the spot. Export the ones you want straight to your Photos library.

Crash detection

GoDash can watch for a collision or hard brake and auto-save the moment, the run-up and the aftermath, without you touching the phone.

Your Privacy Matters

Your recordings, telemetry, and account details stay on your device. GoDash runs no servers that collect your data, and uses no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking. The only things that leave your phone go to the Apple services you choose to use.
