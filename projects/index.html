GoDash

Your whole drive on one dashboard.

An iOS app that turns an iPhone on a car mount into an instrumented dash cam. It records a rolling 10‑minute loop, burns live telemetry into the footage, and runs Apple Maps and Apple Music side by side in split modules so you never have to switch apps while driving.
